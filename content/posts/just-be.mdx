Sometimes, I just feel happy—calm, light, like everything is alright.
But then, there’s this voice that whispers *it won’t last.*
Happiness feels like a passing moment, always just out of reach.

And then, there are days when everything feels heavy.
Slow. Grey.
And the same voice goes silent.
It says *this is forever. You won’t get out of this.*

It’s strange—how happiness feels fleeting,
But sadness feels like it has no end.
Like joy visits, but sorrow stays.

But somewhere deep down, there’s a quieter voice.
A soft friend.
Not offering answers, but just showing up.
*You’re okay*, it says. *You’re not alone*.
And that’s all I need to hear.
Nothing more.
Not the noise of the mind trying to fix, label, or explain.

I’ve spent too long trying to be the one who figures everything out.
Smart. Reflective. Always searching.
But maybe that’s not what matters.
Maybe it’s okay to just feel things, without the need to solve them.

Maybe happiness doesn’t need a reason.
Maybe sadness doesn’t need a solution.
Maybe life isn’t about balance, but about letting things come and go,
Without holding on.

If joy shows up—just let it.
Laugh.
Dance.
Don’t ask how long it’ll last.

If pain shows up—feel it.
Don’t fight it.
And when it’s too much—reach out.
Someone will hold your hand.
Someone will sit with you in the silence.

And when nothing else works,
Play music.
The right song at the right time feels like magic.
No answers. No advice.
Just presence.
Just rhythm.
Just being.

Life doesn’t need to be figured out.
But it gets a little better with some music.
