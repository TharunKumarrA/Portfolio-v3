Have you ever been fascinated by the people who can “Do just about anything”? I’m talking about the folks who can deliver captivating speeches, ace their college courses, and party like there’s no tomorrow. Personally, I have always been fascinated by individuals who are an expert in a particular topic while having a general knowledge of just about anything. What is it that sets them apart from us, the average folks? Well, my friend, the answer to this question will be found in this post.

This article draws inspiration from a thought-provoking tweet by Ali Abdaal (who is a doctor and one of the world’s most-followed productivity experts), wherein he shares valuable insights on enhancing college study habits [1]. In essence, much like how individuals require a specialized toolbox to effectively tackle specific tasks, there exist a set of versatile tools that can greatly benefit a person’s journey in both their professional and personal spheres. These tools are commonly referred to as the “Universal Toolbox”.

Now, let us delve into the contents of this Universal Toolbox one by one. We’ll kick things off with the most obvious and crucial skills that make a huge impact, and then we’ll move on to some super-specific skills that are in high demand in this day and age.

### Communication Skills

I can go on and on forever about the importance of having good communication skills. And hey, it's not just me, every successful person stresses the importance of this skill.

Expressing what’s in our mind is an art, not all can do it as they desire. Some, express through art, some do it by let's say music, but most of us express our feelings, desires, ideas and emotions through communication.

Having a basic understanding of how to communicate better is an absolute game-changer. Some of the key aspects in improving your communication skills are:

- learning how to be an active listener

- some basic ideas on understanding body language

- improving your public speaking skills or presentation skills

These fundamental abilities will propel you forward in every single aspect of your life. The fact that you can talk to an audience at any given time (of course, with some preparation) and the ability to predict the mood or state of a person by observing their body language is absolutely a superpower, that boosts your confidence and self-image.

To start working on your communication skills I would recommend you to read —“How to Talk to Anyone” Book by Leil Lowndes and “What Every BODY is Saying” by Joe Navarro and Marvin Karlins.

### Time Management

Time management is one of the foundational pillars of productivity. Its importance is best expressed by what the world-renowned American trumpeter Miles Davis said,

> Time isn’t the main thing. Time is the only thing.

Having control over time doesn't only make you a better person it also enables you to focus on living a life of your choice — doing things you love and living life as you want. Time management is synonymous with the sail of a ship that a sailor uses to sail through a storm without being swayed away by the winds. I’m pretty sure we all have gone through a period of time in our life when “We had so much to do, while so less time to even start”. This state of us can be explained by the lack of time management.

Alrighty, now let me share some valuable insights on effective time management with you so you can avoid those good old “no time” days. The following points are supported by scientific research from around the world and have been emphasized by numerous successful individuals.

- Prioritize your tasks and activities based on their importance and urgency. This helps you focus on what matters most and avoid getting overwhelmed by less significant tasks. Start your day by identifying your top priorities and creating a to-do list. The Eisenhower Matrix [provide a link to it] is a task management tool that helps you organize and prioritize tasks by urgency and importance. Using the tool, you’ll divide your tasks into four boxes based on the tasks you’ll do first, the tasks you’ll schedule for later, the tasks you’ll delegate, and the tasks you’ll delete.

- Start practising regular exercise, meditation, or taking short breaks throughout the day to recharge and maintain a clear mindset. Poor time management often leads to increased stress levels as deadlines approach, tasks pile up, and you feel overwhelmed. Managing your time effectively helps you stay on top of your responsibilities, reducing stress and promoting a healthier work-life balance.

- Dedicating specific time slots of a day to tackle specific tasks will help you give undivided attention to the work at hand. This will improve your focus and concentration leading to higher-quality work with fewer errors. To achieve this you can minimize distractions around yourself. For example, you can off notifications on your devices, close unnecessary browser tabs, and create a designated workspace free from interruptions.

For more tips on how to prioritize tasks and reduce distractions I will recommend you start reading — *Atomic Habits* by James Clear and *Deep Work* by Cal Newport.

### The ability to say “NO”

This concept is a radical thought for most of us. For most of our lives, we have been conditioned to say “YES” to please people or befriend them. “Can you join us in this event?” “Yes, sure.” , “Would you mind helping me with my work?” “Yes, of course.”

I am not saying we should be selfish and say no to everything. What I am coming to say is that — saying yes without a second thought for everything can become toxic. Many of us had the experience of regretting our decision of committing to something we didn’t genuinely want to do. To avoid these bitter experiences, here are some practical tips to consider:

- Reflect on your priorities and values before responding. Take a moment to evaluate if the request aligns with your personal goals and commitments. Set your boundaries clearly with others.

- Instead of saying “no” directly to the face, you can use the trick of asking, “Is it ok if I say no?”, surprisingly most of the time people accept your “no” without asking for any reason.

- The act of committing to things that matter the most and saying no to other irrelevant things directly improves your ability to take control of your life and can be considered an extension of time management.

### Negotiation and Persuasion Skills

We Indians are the best-known for our negotiating skills. Even if you are a millionaire, you won’t buy a single commodity in the local market without bargaining. But there is a need for us to learn how to apply these negotiation skills in other aspects of life such as in work.

The ability to negotiate in case of a conflict and persuade for one’s gain is absolutely game-changing for anyone. Here are some tips on how to negotiate or persuade anyone to accomplish what we need:

- Developing empathy for the other person is crucial when trying to influence them. Understanding the other person’s perspective and emotions is crucial in both the cases of negotiation and persuasion. Don’t criticize, condemn, or complain. Instead of pointing out flaws and mistakes, focus on understanding others’ perspectives and finding constructive solutions.

- Anchoring refers to the practice of setting the initial offer or reference point in a negotiation. It is recommended to use aggressive anchors to influence the negotiation in your favour. By presenting an extreme or bold proposal, you can shift the range of acceptable outcomes closer to your desired outcome.

- Smile! A smile is a simple yet powerful tool for creating a positive atmosphere and making others feel comfortable and appreciated.

- Make others feel important and respected. Give others a sense of significance by acknowledging their accomplishments, seeking their opinions, and genuinely considering their ideas. This fosters positive relationships and cooperation.

### Financial Literacy

We all aspire to be millionaires in life. But only a few achieve this goal. The goal is clear in our minds, but what about the path? the path to achieving our dream. That's when the idea of FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) comes in. It's a movement started in the late 90s, wherein your passive income generates enough money to take care of your financial needs, so there is no need for you to work daily to earn.

u to work for money — you can either stop working and retire in your bungalow or do the work that you are most passionate about.

This FIRE method provides a goal and a step-by-step procedure that anyone can follow to achieve their financial goals. This will help you with planning the budget, savings, investments, retirement, etc.

If you were to start now to learn about financial literacy, I would suggest these books — The *Psychology of Money* by Morgan Housel and *Rich Dad Poor Dad* by Robert T Kiyosaki and Sharon. Also, the Youtube channel “Ankur Warikoo” is a great starting point.

### Digital Literacy

The internet is a miracle. It's the only thing we refer to as a place when in reality it's just a network of wires and data. Since it is a new place for most of us, many can get lost or take the wrong route.

So having the skills to use technology appropriately, navigate the internet and understand digital tools is essential for research and accessing information. Also, if you are digitally literate means you know what information to trust and what not to on the internet. This implies you can be a lot safer on the internet.

This skill can only be developed over time using the internet, evolving with it. Like a forest-dweller who knows which berry is good and which berry is poisonous, we too will come to know what to trust and what not to.

### Coding & Prompt Engineering

In this age of computing, coding is one of the best leverage talents you can learn. Learning to code will open up a multitude of opportunities. With the rapid advancement of technology and its integration into every aspect of our lives, coding has become an essential skill that transcends industries and professions. Learning to code will empower you with 2 tools — Analytical and critical thinking. These will help you to understand a problem, split it into smaller manageable parts and seek a solution.

However, the recent advancements in AI that are capable of coding make us feel anyone can code. That's not completely true, only people who can properly talk to these AI can only extract the result they want. Here is where the idea of prompt engineering comes in. Prompt engineering, in simple terms, refers to the process of crafting well-defined and specific instructions or queries to elicit desired responses from a language model like ChatGPT. For example, this article you are reading now also used the power of AI to proofread and improvise wherever needed.

Mastering these two will be one of the highest-leverage skills you can learn according to me.

### Embrace feedback

Feedback is the backbone of modern AI tools and a fundamental concept in any system. Just like a magnificent tree that captivates us with its lush-green leaves, extending branches, and beautiful flowers, we often fixate on the visible aspects. Yet, hidden beneath the surface lies the intricate network of roots, silently nourishing and enabling the tree’s growth. Similarly, within any system or organization, there exists a concealed realm of improvement and development where feedback plays a key role.

People of afraid of feedback, literally. Whatever they do, the only opinion that matters to them is theirs. But in reality, feedback from an end-user, client or friend is so important to the development. It's a different lens through which you can see your work.

For example, when I started to write blogs (a month ago when I'm writing this) I was so scared that people may not like what I write or worse no one will read these. Anyways I proceeded to write my first blog and openly I asked many of my friends to give feedback on it. You know what, nothing bad happened conversely, all of them liked it and gave good feedback on it, now I'm sure this one is better than my first one. So, friends embrace feedback.
