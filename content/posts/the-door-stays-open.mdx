I defend my friends a lot when I argue with my parents.

They always ask, “What’s so special about friends? They’re not permanent. They don’t have your back. Do you really think they’ll be there when you’re old?”

Maybe what they’re saying is true to some extent.

But I’ve always believed this line. “A good family member is someone you can consider a friend, while a good friend is someone you consider family.”

From my experience, friendship is one of the most beautiful bonds you can have. I’m not talking about the casual hi-bye kind of friend or the friend you meet over drinks. I’m talking about friends who know you, who you can be yourself around, and who have your back even when you’re not in the room.

Well, why is it special? I have a mom, a dad, a brother, a sister, maybe a wife or a husband. Why can’t they be enough?

I think it’s because friendship is contractless.

You can go years without talking to a cousin, run into them at a wedding, and they’re still your cousin. You can have a bad fight with your mom and she’ll still call you for dinner. These relationships have something that ties you together, even when things aren’t going well.

Of course, that doesn’t mean family is only about obligation. I think the most beautiful family relationships are also friendships in their own way. Maybe that’s why I’ve always loved that line.

But with friends, we picked each other somewhere along the way while we were figuring out life. Nothing really required us to.

At first, you’d think that makes it the weakest bond, right?

But I think that’s what makes it beautiful.

Friendship is like sitting in a room with someone and the door’s always open. You can’t close it to make them stay. They can get up and walk out whenever they want.

And there they are, still sitting with you.

You make the effort to keep in touch when you’re far apart. You show up when things go south. You ask for help when it would be easier to say you’re fine. You keep choosing each other, even when it takes a little more effort than it used to.

So call yourself lucky if you have someone in your life who chooses you when the days are hard, when you’re hard to handle, when you’re a mess or just not yourself.

Someone who lends you their belief when you’ve run out of your own. Someone who stands beside you long enough for bravery to catch up.

But I think there’s something even more beautiful than having people like that in your life.

It’s getting to be that person for someone else.

If you’re even luckier, you’ll have the privilege of being there in their lowest moments, helping carry some of the weight when life gets too heavy.

I know how hard it is to ask for help when you already feel like a burden. And I know how easy it is to convince yourself that you’re troubling someone by reaching out.

But if you’ve ever felt that way about asking me, I want you to know something.

It’s an honor to be there for you, my friend.

To all the friends and loved ones in my life, thank you for standing beside me and still choosing me when life was hard and I was harder.

I don’t know if you’ll choose me forever. Maybe some of us will grow apart, maybe life will take us in different directions. I don’t know.

But for however long we get, thank you for letting me into your life and giving me the privilege of having you in mine.

And I think that’s enough. After all, the door stays open.
