Things become beautiful when you notice them.
People become beautiful when you notice them.

I’m here, sitting on the ground all alone.
The whole ground just for me.
A beautiful view of the mountain.
Goats grazing lazily. Cranes flying in circles. Squirrels darting from tree to tree.

I tried climbing a tree — sat there for a while.
The evening sun was gorgeous, shy behind her clouds.
A soft breeze stirred the air.

What a day.

There’s something special about solitude.
Not being alone, but being with yourself.

You start noticing —
Noticing the world outside you,
Noticing the world inside you.

In these small moments, you realize what you bring into the world.

Try it sometime.
Sit quietly, without screens, not because screens are bad — but because this is good.

Noticing life is good.

*I wrote this while sitting alone on my campus ground during exam break days — when the usual noise of cricket and football was replaced by grazing goats, flying cranes, and a quiet breeze.*
