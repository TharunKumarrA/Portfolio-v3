## Introduction

Let’s talk about Artificial intelligence, shall we? When you hear AI, the first thing that comes to your mind is Humanoid robots taking over the world or in recent times — “ChatGPT”. Anyone from a kid in an old town to a billionaire in his 80s is talking about this. But let me state it clearly here in the first place: Don’t equate ChatGPT with AI. This mistake was made by most of us when we equated the Internet with Social media.

The last observation of mine really fascinates me, let me state why. You see, the moment people hear AI, the first thing that gets hold of them is fear. But I am here to argue that AI is just another new innovation that is no different from technologies that came up in the past. My friend, HISTORY REPEATS ITSELF.

## Understanding AI

Let’s start with understanding what is Artificial intelligence. But to define artificial intelligence we need to define what is intelligence in the first place. Well, the definition of intelligence is — the ability to understand, learn and think. Being a Computer Science student having a little experience with ML and AI I can very well say that AI models can mimic the human mind very well in cases of learning and understanding, but it is not human when it comes to THINKING! Machines cannot Think (at least for now in the ways we do).

Here is an analogy to understand the above statements clearer. Man wanted to travel faster, he invented cars to replace horses. Man wanted to fly, he invented the plane. Although the principle of flying between a bird and a plane is the same, a plane can never be a bird. And having a plane doesn’t kick out the birds from existence.

Similarly, an AI replicates the basic working of the human brain — finding patterns. Our brain is a really sophisticated pattern finder. Why do we need habits? why do we need to connect the dots? It’s because our brain needs to find or analyze patterns to make sense of things.

So, we can see that although AI can learn new stuff, relate data and therefore predict, it can not have rational thoughts or thoughts in the first place because mathematic equations can’t be defined for a thought. We still can’t figure out what is a thought scientifically. So, the possibility of AI taking over humans seems not possible any soon.

## Facing the Fear Factor

These technologies that disrupt industries in ways that were not even possible before are called General Purpose Technologies (GPTs). GPTs have applications in nearly every industry. So many GPTs have changed the course of history. AI although being a game-changer, is not the first game-changer in the industry or the society. Let me list some of the well-known GPTs for you:

The most recent one is the Internet. Yes, the Internet is a General Purpose Technology. The most famous is the Steam Engine — the invention that started the industrial revolution. The control of fire (the methods of creating, handling and manipulating fire) is the first.

The understanding and the mixed feeling about AI we have — are the same as a guy in the 1990s trying to understand the new internet or a factory worker in the 1800s trying to make sense of the new stream engine.

Will AI replace our jobs? The answer is both yes and no. First of all, AI is not going to replace jobs a person who knows how to use AI better will replace many jobs. AI is just another powerful tool that will pave the way forward.
